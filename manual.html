FM-106 Synthesizer

Authentic 6-Voice Roland Juno-106 Firmware for the M-VAVE FM-1

The FM-106 firmware transforms the ultra-portable M-VAVE FM-1 synthesizer into a fully featured, authentic emulation of the classic 1984 Roland Juno-106 analog synthesizer. Engineered with dual-core DSP acceleration, single-precision FPU optimizations, 256 presets (128 Factory + 128 User slots with on-device naming), an authentic 128-step acid sequencer, an interactive CRT oscilloscope, Bluetooth MIDI, and a comprehensive 45-parameter MIDI CC mapping engine with live MIDI Learn.

6 Polyphonic Voices IR3109 Resonant Filter MN3009 BBD Stereo Chorus Vintage Tape & Shimmer 128-Step Acid Sequencer 256 Total Presets Class-Compliant USB Audio

1. Introduction

The FM-106 is an open-source, bare-metal synthesizer firmware developed for the M-VAVE FM-1 hardware. It models the circuit-level behavior of every block in the original Roland Juno-106 synthesizer:

  • Digitally Controlled Oscillators (DCO) with PolyBLEP anti-aliased Sawtooth, Pulse width modulation (manual or LFO driven), Sub-oscillator (-1 octave square), and analog white noise.
  • Voltage Controlled Filter (VCF) modeling the renowned Roland IR3109 / BA662 4-pole 24 dB/oct resonant low-pass ladder filter with self-oscillation and non-linear saturation.
  • High Pass Filter (HPF) with the authentic 4-position discrete resistor network: 0 (Bass Shelf Boost +9.4 dB @ 103 Hz), 1 (Flat), 2 (236 Hz), and 3 (754 Hz).
  • 14-Bit Integer ADSR Envelope Generator derived from the NEC μPD7811G voice microprocessor code with piecewise linear attack and exponential decay/release curves.
  • Dual MN3009 Bucket Brigade Device (BBD) Chorus providing the unmistakable stereo lushness of Chorus I, Chorus II, and the dual I+II vibrato mode.
  • Studio Quality Tape Echo & Shimmer Reverb running on single-precision floating-point hardware for expansive ambient pads and cosmic leads.

5. VCF & HPF Filter Architecture

The low-pass filter is the sonic centerpiece of the Juno-106 sound. The FM-106 implements a digital recreation of the custom Roland IR3109 integrated circuit containing four Operational Transconductance Amplifier (OTA) stages:

  • Self-Oscillation: When RES is elevated beyond ~105, the filter breaks into a pure, singing sinusoidal self-oscillation. With keyboard tracking (KBD) dialed to ~106, the self-oscillating filter tracks the musical keyboard in 1V/octave pitch, functioning as an additional sine wave oscillator!
  • OTA Saturation: High resonance and multi-voice chords smoothly soft-clip through the non-linear hyperbolic tangent ($\tanh$) transfer curves of the differential input pairs, preventing harsh digital clipping and preserving analog warmth.
  • Sub-Sampled Control Voltage Engine: Cutoff frequency calculation is sub-sampled at 11.025 kHz (every 4 audio samples) with linear interpolation, delivering analog-smooth modulation while conserving CPU overhead.

The Authentic 4-Position HPF

Unlike standard digital high-pass filters, the Juno-106 HPF switch utilizes a specialized passive RC network. Position 0 famously engages a bass-boost resonance:

Position On-Screen Label Circuit Behavior & Sonic Character
0 BST (Bass Boost) Engages a low-frequency resonant shelf providing +9.4 dB gain at 103 Hz. Ideal for thunderous Juno basslines and thick synthwave leads.
1 FLT (Flat) Bypasses the high-pass circuit for completely flat, uncolored frequency response down to 10 Hz.
2 236 (236 Hz) 1-pole 6 dB/oct high-pass filter with cutoff at 236 Hz. Gently rolls off sub-bass rumble to let brass and pads sit cleanly in a busy mix.
3 754 (754 Hz) High-pass cutoff at 754 Hz. Thin, reedy, cutting textures reminiscent of harpsichords, clavs, and vintage telephone effects.

6. PolyBLEP DCO & Pulse Width Modulation

Original Juno-106 DCOs utilized an 8253 programmable interval timer to generate reset pulses for analog ramp integrators, ensuring rock-solid tuning with zero phase drift across all 6 voices.

The FM-106 uses PolyBLEP (Polynomial Band-Limited Step) anti-aliasing algorithms to eliminate Nyquist aliasing while retaining razor-sharp analog edges up to the highest MIDI notes:

  • Sawtooth Wave: Rich, full-frequency harmonic spectrum with authentic PolyBLEP edge correction.
  • Pulse / PWM: Continuously variable duty cycle from 50% square down to razor-thin 1% needle pulses. In PWM MD = LFO, the pulse width breathes with the low frequency oscillator. In PWM MD = MAN, Knob 3 directly positions the pulse width.
  • Sub-Oscillator: Flip-flop divider producing a perfect square wave exactly one octave below the fundamental pitch. Controlled with the SUB fader for immense low-end weight.
  • White Noise: Digital pseudo-random linear-feedback shift register (LFSR) emulation delivering analog-style noise for percussive clicks, breath, surf, and wind FX.

7. Dual BBD Chorus, Tape Echo & Shimmer Reverb

Roland MN3009 Stereo BBD Chorus

The Juno-106 chorus is world-famous. The FM-106 models two independent bucket-brigade delay lines with dual complementary anti-phase triangular LFOs:

  • OFF: Chorus completely bypassed for direct, monophonic dry signal.
  • Chorus I: Slow, deep modulation (0.39 Hz, delay sweep 3.8 ms to 5.4 ms). Produces a wide, undulating stereo spread.
  • Chorus II: Faster, shimmering modulation (0.80 Hz, delay sweep 3.2 ms to 6.1 ms). Adds rich movement and motion to electric pianos, bells, and strings.
  • Chorus I+II (Secret Mode): Both buttons engaged simultaneously. LFO speed increases to 8.0 Hz with high depth, producing an iconic lo-fi rotary/vibrato wobble.

Vintage Tape Echo

Accessible on the TAPE edit page (or by cycling FX), this processor simulates magnetic tape saturation, hysteresis, head bump resonance, and mechanical wow/flutter. Select from WARM, VINT, or LOFI tape formulations with real-time drive (CHAR) and delay time (RATE).

Cosmic Shimmer Reverb

Accessible on the REVERB edit page, the Shimmer Reverb combines a multi-tap algorithmic diffuser with dual fractional pitch-shifting delay lines inside the feedback loop. Setting SHIM introduces cascading +1 octave harmonics that bloom into celestial, infinite ambient soundscapes.

8. 128-Step Acid Sequencer

The FM-106 features an onboard 128-step polyphonic and acid sequencer with an interactive piano roll display and per-step parameter automation (P-Locks):

SEQUENCER SHORTCUTS & NAVIGATION
  • PLAY: Start or pause the sequencer playback.
  • REC: Toggle record-arm. Pressing PLAY while armed begins a 4-beat visual metronome count-in (CNT), then records notes and lengths in real-time.
  • SELECT (Turn): Scroll the piano roll viewport through all 128 steps.
  • SELECT (Tap): Clear the note on the currently selected step.
  • SELECT (Hold 500ms): Clear all steps in the sequence.
  • EDIT (Tap): Open Step Note Options for the selected step.
  • EDIT (Hold 500ms): Open Sequencer Global Options.
  • ENV: Toggle Slide (Portamento) flag on current step.
  • LFO: Toggle Accent flag on current step.
  • Knobs 1..4: Directly tweak STEP, NOTE, GATE, and VEL for the current step.

Step Note Options Menu (Tap EDIT)

Highlight any step in the piano roll and tap EDIT to configure advanced step attributes:

  • RATCHET (1..4): Subdivides the step into up to 4 rapid micro-triggers (burst rolls / trap ratchets).
  • TIE (ON/OFF): Ties the step note to the subsequent step, creating legato phrases without re-triggering envelopes.
  • ACCENT (ON/OFF): Increases velocity and filter envelope depth for aggressive Roland TB-303-style punch.
  • SLIDE (ON/OFF): Glides pitch smoothly from the current note to the next step.
  • PROB (0%..100%, MUTE): Sets the firing probability of the step for generative, ever-evolving rhythms.
  • P-LOCKS: Displays the count of automated parameter locks assigned to this specific step.

Sequencer Global Options Menu (Hold EDIT 500ms)

  • STEPS (1..128): Set the loop pattern length.
  • BPM (40..240): Adjust playback tempo (also syncable to external MIDI clock).
  • DIV: Clock division (1/4, 1/8, 1/16, 1/32, triplets).
  • GATE (8..128): Default gate length percentage.
  • SWING (0..100%): MPC-style groove swing.
  • DUP BARS: Automatically duplicates your active bars to double the pattern length.
  • CLR ALL: Instantly resets all steps and parameter locks.

9. Presets & Memory Management

The FM-106 includes 256 preset memory locations arranged in 4 banks of 64 patches:

  • Bank A: Factory A (A11 to A88) — The first 64 original Roland Juno-106 factory presets.
  • Bank B: Factory B (B11 to B88) — The second 64 original Roland Juno-106 factory presets.
  • Bank UA: User A (UA11 to UA88) — 64 fully editable user preset slots stored in on-board flash.
  • Bank UB: User B (UB11 to UB88) — 64 additional user preset slots stored in on-board flash.
Preset Code Format Patches are numbered using authentic Roland 2-digit matrix notation: Bank 1..8, Patch 1..8 (e.g., A11, A12 .. A88). Turn ALGORITHM to switch banks; turn PRESETS to browse patches within the bank.

Saving and Naming Presets (SAVE)

  1. Dial in your sound using the edit pages and faders.
  2. Press SAVE to open the preset destination and naming screen.
  3. Turn SELECT to choose a destination user slot (UA11 to UB88). The screen displays existing patch names or indicates <BLANK SLOT>.
  4. Turn ALGORITHM to move the character cursor across the 15-character name field.
  5. Turn PRESETS to pick characters (letters A-Z, numbers 0-9, spaces, and punctuation).
  6. Press SAVE to commit the preset to non-volatile flash memory. The banner confirms SAVED TO UA...
  7. To delete an existing user preset, press SELECT and confirm when prompted with ARE YOU SURE?.
  8. Press HOME at any time to cancel without saving.

10. Global Setup Menus

Press GLO / OPT to enter the system configuration screen:

MIDI Setup Menu

  • MIDI IN: Set receive channel to ALL (Omni mode) or individual channels 1 through 16.
  • MIDI OUT: Set transmit channel for keyboard performance and sequencer data (1 through 16).
  • SYNC: Choose between OFF (internal clock only), MASTER (sends MIDI 24-PPQN clock and start/stop commands), or SLAVE (syncs tempo and sequencer transport to incoming MIDI clock).
  • LOCAL: ON routes the 27 physical keys to the internal synth engine; OFF disconnects the local keys so they only send MIDI out to external DAWs without triggering local voices.
  • PC IN: ON accepts MIDI Program Change commands from external controllers; OFF ignores external preset switches.
  • CC CONFIG: Highlight and press SELECT to enter the interactive MIDI CC Mapping & Learn menu.

CPU Load Monitor

The bottom section of the Global Menu features a high-precision real-time load monitor displaying CPU0 synthesizer core execution percentage and buffer headroom.

11. MIDI CC Map & Interactive MIDI Learn

The FM-106 features an interactive 45-parameter MIDI Continuous Controller (CC) engine. Every sound design, effects, and sequencer parameter can be controlled over USB MIDI and Bluetooth MIDI:

HOW TO USE MIDI LEARN
  1. Press GLO, select MIDI SETUP, and navigate to CC CONFIG.
  2. Scroll the list using the SELECT encoder to find the parameter you wish to map (e.g. [VCF] CUTOFF).
  3. Press SELECT. The header flashes MIDI LEARN: SEND CC and the red LED pulsates.
  4. Move any knob, slider, or pedal on your external MIDI keyboard or hardware controller.
  5. The FM-106 immediately captures the incoming CC number, updates the map, and saves the new configuration to flash!
  6. Press EDIT at any time to instantly restore the factory default CC for that parameter.
  7. Press HOME to exit back to the synth.

12. Bluetooth Low Energy (BLE) MIDI Guide

The FM-106 features Apple-compliant standard BLE MIDI supporting wireless communication with zero dongles:

1. Peripheral (Device) Mode — Connecting to Mac, iOS, Windows & Android

  1. Enter GLO → BLUETOOTH and ensure ROLE is set to DEVICE.
  2. Turn BLUETOOTH to ON. The top header displays BT.
  3. On macOS: Open Audio MIDI Setup → MIDI Studio → Double click the Bluetooth icon.
  4. On iOS: Use any BLE MIDI utility (e.g. GarageBand, AUM, or Bluetooth MIDI Connect).
  5. Select FM-106 and click Connect. The on-screen indicator switches to bright cyan.

2. Host Mode — Connecting Wireless MIDI Keyboards Directly

  1. In GLO → BLUETOOTH, change ROLE to HOST.
  2. Select [OCT+] SCAN DEVICES to open the live BLE MIDI Scanner.
  3. Place your Bluetooth MIDI controller (e.g., Korg microKEY Air, CME Xkey Air) into pairing mode.
  4. The scanner displays discovered devices with signal strength (dBm).
  5. Highlight your keyboard and press SELECT → CONNECT NOW.
  6. Enable AUTO CONNECT: ON (#1) to have the FM-106 automatically reconnect whenever powered on!

13. USB Audio, MIDI & Web Librarian

Class-Compliant USB Audio (UAC)

The FM-106 streams pristine 16-bit 44.1 kHz stereo digital audio directly over its USB-C connection without requiring drivers. Connect to:

  • macOS & Windows DAWs: Select FM-106 as an input audio device in Ableton Live, Logic Pro, Reaper, FL Studio, or Pro Tools. Record the synth in bit-perfect digital fidelity with zero analog noise or ground hum!
  • iOS & Android: Connect via USB-C to record directly into camera apps or mobile DAWs.

Interactive Web Librarian & SysEx Support

FM-106 includes a standalone visual Web MIDI patch librarian (Librarian.html). Connect your FM-106 over USB, open Librarian.html in Chrome or Edge, and:

  • Inspect and tweak any Juno-106 patch with live Roland sliders.
  • Import standard Roland Juno-106 SysEx (.syx), Jarvik7 (.106), Web JSON, and vintage Roland Juno-106 Tape Cassette audio dumps (.wav).
  • Load curated artist soundsets into User Banks: Tame Impala Anthology, Boards of Canada & Tycho, Vaporwave & Mallsoft, Synthwave / Outrun 1984, Future Classic (LFO Store), and Psychwave & Retrograde.
  • Burn and sync custom user banks directly to hardware SPI flash memory.

14. Troubleshooting & FAQ

Q: My notes stop abruptly and don't decay when I release the keys.

A: Check your VCA Mode on PAGE 8 (FX). Ensure VCA is set to ENV (Envelope mode). If set to GATE, the envelope release curve is bypassed in favor of raw organ-style gating.

Q: How do I recover or update the firmware if the device doesn't boot?

A: The FM-1 hardware features a hardware bootloader rescue mode:

  1. Power off the FM-1.
  2. Hold down OCT - while powering on.
  3. The screen displays the hardware recovery updater. Connect via USB and run python3 tools/fm1_install.py build/fm106.fwsc.

Q: Why is there no sound coming from the 3.5mm headphone jack?

A: Check the MASTER analog volume potentiometer on the top-right corner of the panel. Ensure LEVEL on the FX page is turned up, and verify that LOCAL is set to ON in the MIDI Setup menu.
